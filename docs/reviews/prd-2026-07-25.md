# Alder 0.1 PRD review — 2026-07-25

Verdict: approved for implementation with explicit gates. The product thesis,
type-level leakage model, immutable fit protocol, error algebra, module intent,
and backend boundary are coherent. Eight specification defects were corrected
in `PRD.json`; four scaffold defects and one deliberately open decision must be
resolved or explicitly deferred before the affected public surfaces are
considered complete.

## Review basis

- Canonical `PRD.json`, including decisions D1–D22 and open decision O7.
- The initial `alder-kernel` and `alder-laws` implementation at commit
  `25eb0a7438cd56f58de5b54e73309b34f7f88b56`.
- The declared sbt module graph, dependency policy, strict compiler flags, and
  JVM/Scala.js/Scala Native cross-build.
- The frozen type-level commitments: use roles, explicit error unions with
  attributed failures, preparation scopes, framework-owned audit construction,
  and capability evidence.

## Corrected specification findings

1. **Blocker — cyclic module boundary.** The PRD placed
   `FeatureMap.crossFitted` in the kernel-facing constructor vocabulary while
   requiring a `Resampler` owned by `alder-data`, which itself depends on the
   kernel. The ratified design keeps the dependency one-way:
   `alder-data` provides the `Resampler` protocol and an extension on
   `FeatureMap.type`.
2. **Blocker — refit incompatibility.** `FeatureMap.fit` accepts
   `U <: Use.Fit`, but `FoldEncoder.fit` accepted only `Use.Train`. A
   cross-fitted `Use.Refit` fit could not perform its required final serving
   fit without unsafe retagging. `FoldEncoder.fit` now preserves
   `U <: Use.Fit`.
3. **Major — law assigned to the wrong abstraction.** Seed allocation is a fit
   protocol concern and cannot be observed by an already-fitted `Pipe`. It is
   now stated only under Transform composition; Pipe associativity covers
   output and attributed first failure.
4. **Major — mutable codec payload.** The immutable fitted-artifact thesis was
   inconsistent with a public `Array[Byte]` codec boundary. The PRD now requires
   `IArray[Byte]`.
5. **Blocker — incomplete resamplers cannot drive cross-fitting.** The initial
   constructor vocabulary accepted an undifferentiated resampler, but
   rolling-origin leaves its initial analysis window without an assessment
   appearance. D19 introduces `CompleteResampler`; only exactly-once assessment
   schemes such as K-fold and grouped K-fold may drive `crossFitted`.
6. **Blocker — FoldEncoder and rowwise composition lost provenance.**
   `FoldEncoder.State` was fitted but unaudited, `encode` returned a naked error,
   and `andThen` had no channel for analysis-row encode failures. Meanwhile the
   FeatureMap rowwise algebra admitted an arbitrary fitted Pipe without its
   mandatory audit or proof of training independence. D20 makes encoder state
   `Trained`, retains `Failure`, exposes run failures during fit, and limits 0.1
   rowwise postprocessing to total `mapOutput`.
7. **Blocker — cross-fitting lineage schema was underspecified.** D21 resolves
   O6 with a compact plan receipt: policy-tagged resampler and assignment
   fingerprints, split seed, ordered per-fold analysis/assessment fingerprints
   and fitted-state lineage, plus final all-row serving lineage. Production
   lineage contains no RowId lists.
8. **Blocker — law-module ownership formed a dependency cycle.** The PRD put
   `MetricTests` and `SpaceTests` in kernel-only `alder-laws`, even though
   `Metric` and `Space` are owned by downstream modules, while the tuning slice
   depended on the supposedly complete core law kit. D22 keeps kernel contract
   laws in `alder-laws` and publishes downstream `alder-metrics-laws` and
   `alder-tune-laws` artifacts that depend in the lawful direction.

## Scaffold findings that gate downstream implementation

1. **Blocker — composition is tree-shaped, not plan-shaped.**
   `ThenTransform.fit` allocates child contexts with local ordinals 0 and 1.
   Consequently `(a.andThen(b)).andThen(c)` assigns different leaf stage paths
   and seeds from `a.andThen(b.andThen(c))`. Nested audit children and
   preparation lineage are likewise parenthesization-shaped. This contradicts
   the PRD's normalized-plan invariant and is not covered by the current law
   suite.
2. **Blocker — plan fingerprint is absent from seed derivation.** The PRD binds
   child seeds to root seed, normalized plan fingerprint, and stable stage
   ordinal. The scaffold derives them only from the current seed and local
   ordinal.
3. **Major — the initial kernel is intentionally incomplete.** Required safe
   algebra rows still need tracker-backed implementations and laws:
   Transform→FeatureMap, FeatureMap→total `mapOutput`, Transform→Learner,
   `FeatureMap.inputOnly`, `FeatureMap.crossFitted`, and
   `FoldEncoder.andThen`.
4. **Major — codec ownership mismatch.** The scaffold still exposes
   `Array[Byte]`; it must be changed before the capability surface is treated as
   frozen.

## Soundness assessment

The central preparation-scope argument is sound: `Reusable` is stronger than
`LearnerReady`, and the safe API correctly refuses to certify a second fitted
preprocessor over pooled out-of-fold rows. The explicit escape route—fit the
downstream transform inside each encoder fold—matches the transitive dependency
law. The separation of statistical policy from numerical backends is also
coherent and keeps backend-specific types out of the kernel.

The PRD remains deliberately incomplete only at O7, the possible future
contract for fallible or pre-fitted rowwise postprocessing. O7 is explicitly
outside 0.1 because total `mapOutput` covers the safe rowwise surface without a
training-independence receipt. O6 is resolved by D21 and its executable
cross-fitting lineage schema.

## Ratified artifact and implementation update

The canonical PRD digest is recorded in mote after every ratified correction.
D20 and the safe kernel composition slice were subsequently checked against
the strict compiler policy and exercised on JVM, Scala.js, and Scala Native.
The composition suite covers exact `Reusable` preservation, RowId/target/meta
preservation, normalized stage paths and seeds across association,
FoldEncoder state audits and failure provenance, terminal learner composition,
and compile-negative exclusion of the unsafe algebra rows.

D21 was then implemented by `alder-data`'s `FeatureMap.crossFitted` extension.
Instrumented laws inspect the actual RowIds visible to every fold state, verify
own-row exclusion and exactly-once original-order OOF assembly, distinguish the
final all-row serving fit, preserve `Use.Refit`, and retain attributed fold
failures. The production receipt stores only policy-tagged fingerprints,
counts, seeds, and child lineage—not RowId lists.
